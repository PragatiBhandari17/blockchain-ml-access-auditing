Interpretation: the baseline's fixed 10s-interval rule flags
'slow_low' attackers (paced just above the threshold) at a rate
close to zero, because each individual request legitimately
satisfies (now - last) >= interval. The ML model instead looks at
aggregate behaviour (request volume, keyword diversity, policy-fail
rate) over a rolling window, so it assigns this attacker a
substantially higher risk score than ordinary users even though no
single request would trip the paper's rule.
